# Project Charter - NDC-01 Mission-Critical Cooling Modernization

## Business context
NovaTech Data Centers (NDC) is a fictional mid-sized colocation and digital-infrastructure provider. NDC-01 is a regional facility serving approximately 80 enterprise customers, with 420 installed racks, approximately 350 occupied racks and a modeled critical IT load up to 800 kW. The facility operates continuously and depends on cooling capacity, redundancy and thermal margin to sustain IT operations.

## Problem statement
The 2026 baseline evidence indicates progressive deterioration of the legacy cooling infrastructure. The seeded reference realization produces 13 equipment failures, 121.75 accumulated equipment-downtime hours, 66.75 hours outside the intended N+1 redundancy state, 4.75 hours of cooling-capacity deficit, 72.22% preventive-maintenance compliance, weighted COP 2.809, modeled PUE 1.551 and annual cooling O&M cost of EUR 425,432.79. Equipment downtime is not treated as equivalent to IT-service downtime.

## Project objective
Select and deliver a targeted modernization that improves cooling-service resilience, redundancy visibility, maintainability, energy performance and operating economics while avoiding the cost and disruption of complete plant replacement.

## Authorized project
- Selected alternative: targeted reliability and efficiency modernization.
- Authorized funding: EUR 360,000.
- Planned duration: 32 weeks.
- Delivery approach: predictive for design/procurement/installation/commissioning; iterative for controls, monitoring, alarm rationalization and dashboards.

## Major scope
VFDs; selected instrumentation; control optimization; condition monitoring; BMS/data visibility; alarm rationalization; selected reliability interventions; maintenance-process redesign; integration; verification; validation; training and transition.

## Governance principle
Targets are defined as inputs. Results are derived from generated evidence. A failed requirement remains a failed requirement; the simulation seed or evidence shall not be modified to force acceptance.
