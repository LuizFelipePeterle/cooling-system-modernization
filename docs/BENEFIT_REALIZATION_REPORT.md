## Interpretation discipline
Cooling-equipment downtime is not IT-service downtime. Cooling-service availability is derived from available cooling capacity versus required cooling demand. Capacity-deficit exposure is therefore separated from asset-level reliability. Direct O&M savings are monetized; avoided customer outages are not included in payback.
