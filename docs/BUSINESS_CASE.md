# Business Case - NDC-01 Targeted Cooling Modernization

## Decision problem
Management must determine whether continued operation of the legacy cooling plant represents acceptable lifecycle and operational exposure, whether full replacement is justified, or whether a targeted modernization can produce sufficient resilience, energy and maintenance benefit at lower capital and transition risk.

## Alternatives
| Alternative | Indicative CAPEX | Assessment |
|---|---:|---|
| Continue as-is | EUR 0 | Preserves current reliability, redundancy and cost exposure. |
| Full cooling-plant replacement | ~EUR 900k | Highest renewal potential but highest CAPEX and implementation disruption. |
| Controls and instrumentation only | ~EUR 130k | Improves visibility/efficiency but only partially addresses reliability and maintainability. |
| Targeted modernization | EUR 360k | Selected balance of reliability, controls, monitoring, maintainability, energy and implementation risk. |

## Seeded reference benefit result
The v6.1 reference realization is not hard-coded as a target. It emerges from the frozen model assumptions and seed.

- Baseline cooling O&M: EUR 425,432.79/year.
- Post-modernization cooling O&M: EUR 357,924.84/year.
- Direct annual O&M saving: EUR 67,507.95/year.
- Direct O&M reduction: 15.87%.
- Authorized CAPEX: EUR 360,000.
- Simple payback using direct O&M savings only: 5.33 years.

No avoided IT-outage value or Expected Annual Loss reduction is monetized in the base payback. Operational-risk reduction is reported separately to avoid double counting and unsupported revenue-loss claims.
