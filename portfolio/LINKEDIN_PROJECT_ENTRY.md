**Title**  
Industrial Cooling System Reliability & Energy Efficiency Modernization - Engineering Case Study

**Description**  
Independent simulated engineering case study integrating Project Management and Systems Engineering across problem analysis, stakeholder needs, requirements, architecture, project planning, procurement, risk and change control, integration, verification, validation, transition and benefit realization.

The case uses governed stochastic operational data and reproducible Python/PostgreSQL evidence to evaluate availability, reliability, maintainability, energy performance and lifecycle operating cost without forcing realized KPIs to predetermined story values.
