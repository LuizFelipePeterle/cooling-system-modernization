# Decision Gates

## G0 — Business Case Approval
Entry: quantified problem, baseline measures, candidate solution classes.
Exit: selected solution class and authorized concept study.

## G1 — System Requirements Review (SRR)
Entry: stakeholder needs, draft system requirements, measurement plan.
Exit: approved and traceable requirements baseline; V&V methods assigned.

## G2 — Preliminary Design Review (PDR)
Entry: architecture, interface definitions, preliminary hazard/risk analysis.
Exit: architecture judged feasible; major interfaces allocated; residual risks acceptable.

## G3 — Critical Design Review (CDR)
Entry: detailed design, procurement specifications, updated risk register.
Exit: design complete enough for implementation/procurement.

## G4 — Test Readiness Review (TRR)
Entry: approved procedures, calibrated measurement chain, test configuration frozen.
Exit: evidence that test can be executed safely and validly.

## G5 — Operational Readiness Review (ORR)
Entry: verified system, training complete, rollback/support plan ready.
Exit: authorization for operational validation.

## G6 — Closure & Transition
Entry: acceptance evidence, outstanding actions dispositioned, handover package complete.
Exit: formal transition, closure, benefits owner assigned.
