# Risk Management Plan

## Probability
1: <10%
2: 10-29%
3: 30-49%
4: 50-69%
5: >=70%

## Impact
Use the worst credible consequence among cost, schedule, technical performance,
operations/safety, and stakeholder/business impact.

1: <1% BAC / <3 days / negligible
2: 1-3% BAC / 3-7 days / minor
3: >3-6% BAC / 1-2 weeks / moderate
4: >6-10% BAC / >2-4 weeks / major milestone or acceptance threatened
5: >10% BAC / >4 weeks / project objective or critical requirement threatened

## Severity
1-4 Low
5-9 Moderate
10-15 High
16-25 Critical
